Money and trade plan

Enter your capital and pick a risk level: the page splits it into risk per trade, a daily loss limit and total open risk, then simulates thousands of trade sequences to show the chance of drawdowns. Pick a symbol and direction: it suggests a stop loss from the nearest swing high/low and from volatility, take profits as R multiples, and works out the size, margin and the adverse move to stop-out, so one trade cannot blow the account.
