Read-only. This page reads closed-trade history from MT5 (demo accounts only by default) and never places, modifies or closes orders. Past results do not guarantee future results; not investment advice.

Trades are assembled from the fills that share a position ID; net profit includes profit, commission, fees and swap. R is net profit divided by the amount lost if the stop loss set at entry is hit (calculated by MT5, order_calc_profit). These are signal, demo-account and shadow-run figures, not real-money results, a promise of profit or investment advice.
