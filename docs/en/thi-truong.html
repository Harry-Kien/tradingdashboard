"Today" is vs. the previous trading day’s close, "Week" vs. the week’s opening price, "1h" vs. the price about 60 minutes ago. Today’s range: the marker is the current price between the day’s low and high; "% ADR" is today’s range relative to the 14-day average range (above 100% is a high-volatility day). Forex spread is in pips; CFD spread is in minimum price increments. Click a symbol name to open its chart, ★ to watch it, "Calc" to open the position size calculator.

Prices: MT5 ticks, read continuously (by default 20 times per second) and pushed to the browser over Server-Sent Events as soon as a new price arrives. Contract specs, margin and price levels are re-read every 30 seconds; correlation every 10 minutes. These are signal data, demo accounts and shadow runs, not real-money results, a promise of profit or investment advice.
